The Production Handoffs
Keep the details with the job
Drawing quantities, slab placement, approvals, work orders, and payments each have a place in the workflow.
📝

Drawing to Estimate

Bring room quantities, edge lengths, and cutout counts from the drawing into an estimate at your uploaded rates.

🪨

Slab to Preview

Move a piece on its slab and its veining updates in the textured view. Check the match by eye.

✅

Design to Approval

Send saved CNC layout options for approval. Customers review the 3D and approve an option or request a change.

🛠️

Template to CNC Prep

Import the templater's DXF into CNC prep. Keep arcs, cutouts, and drill holes with the imported pieces.

📱

Office to Field

Give crews work-order items, site notes, checklists, and customer sign-off in the web-based field app. A connection is required.

💳

Invoice to Payment

Give customers an invoice link for online card or bank payment, and track the job's paid amount and balance.

Features
Tools for the production spine
Drawing → auto-arranged slabs → textured 3D customer approval → CNC-ready DXF export → online payment. Plus the tools that keep the office and field crew moving.
✏️

Countertop Drawing

Draw countertop shapes with edge profiles, cutouts, backsplashes, waterfalls, and aprons. See square feet per piece.

🪨

Auto-Arranged Slabs

Stone Navigator auto-arranges slabs in the drawing's Nesting tab: it places pieces on matching-material slabs, adds seams to oversized pieces, and reports when more stone is needed.

👁️

Textured 3D Customer Approval

The 3D uses the actual slab photo at each piece's nested position. Match veins by eye, then send saved design options for customer approval from CNC prep.

🛠️

CNC Prep & DXF Export

Import your templater's text DXF files, place pieces on slabs by hand, and export CNC-ready DXF for third-party CAM software. Export files are checked against the source before download.

📋

Drawing-Based Estimates

Import room quantities, edge lengths, and cutout counts from the drawing. Build branded estimates with your uploaded rates and share a customer link.

💳

Invoices & Online Payment

Turn an estimate into an invoice, set a payment schedule, and track the paid amount and balance. Customers pay online by card or bank transfer through the invoice link.

📆

Scheduling & Job Boards

Book appointments with job-site addresses and assignees. Move deals and production jobs through your shop's stages, with readiness and blocker details.

📱

Web-Based Field Crew App

Crews view job details and work orders, tick checklists, add photos and notes, and collect customer sign-off. The field app needs a connection.

📇

Customer Records

Store customer names, company details, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, and notes. Search your contact list when you need a record.

🤖

Automations

Appointment confirmations and reminders are set up once by us. Your shop uses Stone Navigator without managing another app.

📸

Reviews, Social & Reputation

Completion-triggered review requests and optional job-site social posts are set up once by us. Your shop handles the job in Stone Navigator without touching another app.

📊

Shop Dashboard & Pricing

See sales and collections against goals, active jobs, pending estimates, and unpaid invoices. Upload material prices and labour rates from spreadsheets.
